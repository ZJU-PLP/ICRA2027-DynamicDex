# Physical-data scope

The Basket aggregate table records the 15-trial hardware result reported in the paper: DynamicDex Full-16 with wrist admittance, 10 static and 5 moving trials. Contact retention and peak rotation are means of trial-level metrics within each condition. Individual trial logs were not supplied with the aggregate confirmation.

sensor_capture_summary.csv is a separate set of independent 10-s tactile/wrist captures used for Fig. 6. The torque values are uncompensated tool-frame means; marker displacement is optical image motion, not calibrated force. Camera and wrist streams were not hardware-synchronized, and the captures do not replace the 15-trial aggregate.
