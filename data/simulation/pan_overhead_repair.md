# Pan overhead-grasp tactile replay

This replay is the verified Pan overhead-grasp repair recording. It is an engineering diagnostic with a fixed wrist and finger-load feedback; it is separate from the learned-policy formal evaluation and from the four common-Home presentation recordings.

The self-contained HTML viewer provides a play button and time slider. Each packet updates the recorded RGB scene, four optical tactile panels, payload slider position, measured object CoM, wrist wrench, and phase label. The tactile panels are visual overlays from the recorded packet stream; they are not calibrated force maps.

Reported repair check: 0.800 kg total mass, approximately ±50 mm payload travel, 100% required-contact retention during the hold, 4.57 mm maximum relative translation, and 8.04° maximum rotation. The recording is a two-run engineering verification, not a success-rate estimate.
